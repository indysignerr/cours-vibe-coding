## Contest 1 · a landing page under constraints

Announced at the start of the hour, week of 5 October. Deadline: **the same day, 23:59**, on the Submit page of the site. One submission per person. You may keep editing it until the deadline.

## The three constraints

1. **A landing page for a real student initiative at Albert School**, existing or invented, with a named person it is for.
2. **Zero images.** Type, colour and layout only. Not even an icon.
3. **Everything in under 80 words.** Count them. A word over is a constraint missed.

## How it is scored

The grid is on the Contests page and has not changed. Two lines are frozen at full marks for everyone this time, because we have not taught them yet: "Holds at 375 pixels" and "Loading, empty and error states". "Typography" is scored, but only on hierarchy: no pairing expected yet.

The gate still applies: if the URL does not open on a judge's phone, the project is not scored.

## Rules

- Your repository must be public, and its first commit must come after this announcement.
- Using an agent is the subject. Submitting someone else's work is the only fraud.
- Thirty seconds of explanation at the next session. If you cannot say what you built, you lose the Ownership points.

Winner announced at the start of session 05, with one sentence on every project submitted.
